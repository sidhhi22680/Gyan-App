3. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only in these cases:

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf under contract, such as hosting and login (Google Firebase).
  • Legal reasons, when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.
  • Business transfers, such as a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.
  • Advertising partners (Google AdMob), only once ads are switched on.
  • With your consent or at your direction.

Other players can see your display name, avatar and score on the leaderboard, and your display name and chat messages in games you join.

Third-party services used in the App:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Fonts — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising, only once ads are switched on) — policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Advertising

Once ads are switched on, the App shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may use the Android advertising ID, IP address and device and usage information to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads). The App is not directed to children under 18 and we do not knowingly show personalised ads to them. We do not sell your personal data.

4. App permissions

The App requests a permission only when the related feature is used, and you can deny or revoke it in your device settings. Some features may stop working without it.

  • Microphone — only to record voice messages in online games. A voice message is saved with the chat messages and shown to the people in that room.
  • Advertising ID (Android) — only once ads are switched on, used by Google AdMob as described in section 3.
  • Notifications — optional; the App may ask to show an alert for a new friend request. You can deny it and everything else still works.
  • The App does not request camera, location, contacts.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data only as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App, meet legal duties, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

Chat messages, photos and voice messages stop being shown in a room after 48 hours; copies may stay in our database until they are deleted as described below.

How to delete your account and data

  • In the App: open the ⋮ menu → Help & Support → Delete account, enter your password, tick the confirmation box and tap “Permanently delete my account”. This deletes your login account and your leaderboard entry. Messages you posted in chat or game rooms are not removed by this button; to have them deleted too, email us (see below).
  • By web request: visit delete-account.html or email shreesitaram9988@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”.

We delete the account data, and the room messages you ask us to remove, within 30 days of a verified request. We may keep a minimal record of a ban or abuse report to protect other players. Some records may be kept longer where law requires, and backups are overwritten on a regular cycle.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.
